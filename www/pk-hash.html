PK hash calculator

Caliptra validates firmware signatures against SHA-384 hashes of the vendor and owner public keys, which are programmed into fuses or OTP during manufacturing. Enter the keys to compute both hashes.

Hash construction

Vendor PK hash is two levels deep. Each vendor ECC and MLDSA key is hashed with SHA-384, the per-key digests are packed into an ImageVendorPubKeyInfo descriptor, and that descriptor is hashed with SHA-384. The descriptor is 1736 bytes: a 4-byte header (version, key type, count) and 4 ECC digest slots of 48 bytes, then a 4-byte header and 32 PQC digest slots of 48 bytes. The four MLDSA digests fill the first PQC slots and the rest stay zero. LMS keys are not included.

Owner PK hash is one level. The owner ECC key (96 bytes) and MLDSA key (2592 bytes) are concatenated into ImageOwnerPubKeys (2688 bytes), which is hashed with SHA-384.

Load test keys fills every field with the test keys from caliptra-sw (caliptra-image-fake-keys). The page loads with them filled in.
