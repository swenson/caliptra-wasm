Test CA ML-DSA-87 and ECC P-384

A certificate authority that runs in this page, for provisioning Caliptra device identities. It checks certificate requests, issues certificates, signs TBS certificates for Caliptra to assemble, and verifies certificate chains, with a post-quantum ML-DSA-87 root and an ECC P-384 root.

Certificate authorities

Both roots are generated from the fixed values shown here, so they are the same in every browser, and issuing the same certificate twice gives the same bytes. The private keys are public, so anyone can issue these certificates. Use them to exercise provisioning flows, and do not rely on them to trust a device.

Verify a certificate chain

The chain must end at one of the test CAs or at a self-signed certificate. Each signature, issuer name and key identifier is checked. Revocation is not checked, and validity dates are shown but not compared with today's date, because Caliptra's own certificates carry fixed dates.
